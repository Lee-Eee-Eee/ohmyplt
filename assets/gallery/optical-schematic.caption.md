Conceptual arrangement for two-channel optical transmittance measurement. The beam splitter sends one optical path through the sample to the signal detector (S), while the other reaches the reference detector (R). Solid amber arrows show optical paths; dashed blue arrows show readout data. Transmittance is normalized as T = (S/R)/(S0/R0). All readings are dark-corrected, and S0/R0 is the baseline ratio acquired in the reference state with the sample removed. This normalization assumes a stable source spectral shape and relative channel response, with linear detector response across sample and reference measurements. Component sizes, spacing, and ray widths are schematic.
